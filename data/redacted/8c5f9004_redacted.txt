Handling of Personal Data

Classification
This pack carries an Internal — Confidential marking and holds directory-level personal data comprising names, corporate email addresses and mobile numbers.

Distribution
Circulation is limited to named recipients with a defined business need. Onward forwarding, printing and upload to third-party or generative AI tools are to be avoided.

Sample data notice
The role holder details shown are synthetic and have been generated for illustration. Live records are to be sourced from the HR system of record before operational use.

Retention
Superseded versions are to be withdrawn once a revised pack is issued; retention beyond the current reporting cycle is not anticipated.
