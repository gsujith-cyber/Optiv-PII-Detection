1. Purpose and objectives

Cadence Financial Group, Inc. ([PERSON_1] “Group") accepts risk as an inherent consequence of providing investment banking, asset
management and consumer banking services. This policy establishes [PERSON_1] Group-wide framework through which risk is identified,
assessed, treated, monitored and reported, so that risk-taking is deliberate, understood, and remains within [PERSON_1] appetite set by [PERSON_1]
[PERSON_2] of Directors.

This policy is [PERSON_1] apex document of [PERSON_1] Group tisk policy hierarchy. All subordinate risk policies, standards and procedures must be
consistent with it. Where a subordinate document conflicts with this policy, this policy prevails. Interpretation questions are directed to
[PERSON_1] policy author, [PERSON_2] ([EMAIL_9], +44 20 7555 0171),

1.1 Objectives

1. Protect capital, liquidity and earnings by ensuring exposures remain within approved appetite and regulatory limits.
2. Enable informed risk-taking through a consistent, comparable view of risk across business lines and geographies.
3. Establish clear accountability for every material risk, with a single named owner at an appropriate level of seniority.
4. Ensure regulatory compliance with prudential, conduct and data-protection obligations in each jurisdiction.

5. Protect customers and market integrity by embedding conduct considerations into risk decisions,

6. Safeguard reputation by identifying and managing risks to stakeholder confidence.

7. Support resilience so [PERSON_1] Group can absorb severe but plausible shocks and continue important business services.
8. Promote a healthy risk culture in which concerns are raised early, escalated without fear, and acted upon.

1.2 Principles

PRINCIPLE STATEMENT

Risk ownership Risk is owned by [PERSON_1] business that creates it. Ownership cannot be delegated to a control function or to a third party.
Proportionality The depth of assessment, control and reporting is proportionate to [PERSON_1] materiality and complexity of [PERSON_1] risk.
Independence The second and third lines have direct, unrestricted access to [PERSON_3] and are free from business pressure.
Transparency Risks are reported completely and without filtering. Understating or delaying a risk report is a breach of this policy.
Forward focus The framework considers emerging and prospective risks, not only realised losses and historical events.
[PERSON_9] taxonomy, scoring scale and definitions apply Group-wide so that risks aggregate meaningfully
Substance over form Assessments reflect actual exposure and actual control operation, not documented intent.
